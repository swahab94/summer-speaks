I was raised in chaos, but I was not created to stay there.

My story is not polished because my life was not easy, but it is powerful because I am still here, still growing, and still choosing purpose.

I grew up surrounded by abuse, poverty, violence, and instability. I endured serious physical abuse, experienced foster care, lived through homelessness even while I was in high school, survived domestic violence, struggled with addiction, and carried the weight of being in and out of custody. I know what it feels like to be judged by your past, counted out by systems, and left to survive with almost no support.

But my life changed when I entered rehabilitation and began to believe that I could become someone different. I went to community college, worked hard, and excelled academically while raising three children as a single mother. I earned multiple awards, became a college graduation speaker, was accepted into all 13 universities I applied to, and now attend one of the world’s most prestigious colleges.

I do not speak from theory alone. I speak from lived experience, healing, accountability, faith, education, motherhood, and the courage it takes to keep going.
